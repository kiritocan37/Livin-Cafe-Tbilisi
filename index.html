About Livin Café

Where conscious dining meets breathtaking views

Perched atop the Livin Hotel at 44 Samreklo Street in the heart of Old Tbilisi's Avlabari district, Livin Café offers the best 360° rooftop terrace view of Tbilisi City and the iconic Holy Trinity Cathedral. We are more than just a restaurant — we are a movement for conscious, healthy, and cruelty-free living.

"To inspire a conscious and healthy lifestyle through balanced, fresh, and cruelty-free food."
  • ✓NO eggs, NO meat, NO poultry, NO seafood
  • ✓100% Halal food
  • ✓Fresh & cruelty-free daily
  • ✓Caring for our planet
